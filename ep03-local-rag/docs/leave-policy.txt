Leave Policy

Every full-time employee gets 24 paid leave days per year, plus public holidays.
Unused days do not carry over past March 31st.

Sick leave is separate: up to 10 days per year with no approval needed, just a
message to your manager before 10 AM. For leaves longer than 3 days, HR needs a
doctor's note.

Leave requests go through the portal, and your manager approves them. Apply at
least two weeks ahead for planned vacations.
