Office Wi-Fi Guide

The office network is called BlueOffice-5G. The guest network is BlueOffice-Guest
and its password rotates every Monday; the current one is printed on the
whiteboard in the kitchen.

If your laptop keeps dropping the connection, forget the network and rejoin it.
If that fails, the router in meeting room B reboots from the red switch on its
left side. Wait ninety seconds before reconnecting.
