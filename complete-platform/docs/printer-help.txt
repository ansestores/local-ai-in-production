Printer Troubleshooting

The main printer is on the 2nd floor next to the coffee machine. Its queue name
is FLOOR2-LASER.

Paper jams: open the front tray, pull the paper in the direction of printing,
never backwards. Toner warnings can be ignored for about 500 pages.

If a print job is stuck, cancel it from the queue on your own machine first.
Turning the printer off does not clear the queue - the job comes back.
